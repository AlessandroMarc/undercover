# AI Forward Deployed Engineer: The AI Architect & FDE Course

## UnderCover Agent: the Travel Insurance Platform

![An alien in disguise joins a line of travelers](assets/undercover.jpg)

This is the repo that accompanies the course. UnderCover is a fictional travel insurer. Over the three weeks we build its claims platform and an AI agent that handles claims.

- **Week 1 Day 1** is in the `setup/` folder.
- **Week 1 Day 2** is in the `labs/` folder.
- **From Week 1 Day 3 onwards** we work in the entire repo.

The installation steps for each day are in [setup/INSTRUCTIONS.md](setup/INSTRUCTIONS.md).
