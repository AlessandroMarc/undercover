# Brand

## Fonts

From Google Fonts:

- **Headings**: Archivo
- **Body**: Instrument Sans
- **Accent (a tagline or a quote)**: Instrument Serif
- **Code, numbers and identifiers**: JetBrains Mono

## Colors

- **Primary**: Electric Blue `#2962FF`, white text
- **Secondary**: Cyan `#22D3EE`, Ink text
- **Tertiary**: Lavender `#B197FC`, Ink text
- **Information**: Mint `#3DD6A0`, Ink text
- **Highlight or alert**: Coral Red `#FF7A7A`, Ink text
- **Ink on light backgrounds**: Ink `#0B1020`
- **Ink on dark backgrounds**: Frost `#EDF1F7`

Use Electric Blue, Cyan and Lavender when three things sit side by side, such as a claim's states or chart series. Add Mint fourth and Coral Red fifth.

## Surfaces

- **Light mode**: page background `#F3F1EA`, white panels and cards
- **Dark mode**: page background `#070B14`, panels and cards `#0D1424`

Panels are rounded with no outline. Cyan, Lavender and Mint are too bright for small text, so darken them for headings and body text on light backgrounds and lighten them on dark ones. On dark backgrounds use `#7C9CFF` in place of Electric Blue.

## Avoid

- Borders on one edge of a panel, and thin lines across the top of the page
- Heavy use of gradient fills

## Tone

Plain business English in US spelling. Short labels, no jargon and no exclamation marks. Money is in US dollars, such as $1,250.00.
